Winter squash: store it right.
4 RULES · SWIPE →
2/5
NATURAL ERA
RULE 1
50–55°F
Skip the fridge.
Whole squash breaks down fast below 50°F. A cool room keeps it better than the refrigerator.
Source: University of Maine Cooperative Extension@thenatural.era
3/5
NATURAL ERA
RULE 2
Dry + airy
Let it breathe.
Aim for 50–70% humidity with good airflow. Paper bags or cardboard boxes work. Try a basement or a cool spare room.
Source: University of Maine Cooperative Extension@thenatural.era
4/5
NATURAL ERA
RULE 3
No apples
Keep fruit away.
Apples, pears and other ripening fruit give off ethylene gas, which shortens squash storage life.
Source: University of Maine Cooperative Extension@thenatural.era
5/5
NATURAL ERA
RULE 4
3–6 mo
Months, not days.
Hubbard and buttercup can keep 3 to 6 months. Store only unblemished squash and check often. Save this for your next market haul.
Source: University of Maine Cooperative Extension@thenatural.era
